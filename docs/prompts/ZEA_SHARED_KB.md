# Zea AI Family — Shared Knowledge Base

Shared by inbound and outbound agents. Contains product/business reference material, not conversation scripts or authorization to execute actions. Product scope below comes from the supplied company draft; current plan and implementation details require confirmation.

## Company and ecosystem
Zea AI Family is the business AI product ecosystem of URL Factory Private Limited. The four products are ZeaCRM, Zea Voice, Zea Play, and Zea Brain. Each serves a different business need. A business may use one or several, depending on its requirements and configured integrations.

## ZeaCRM
Category: CRM and business automation.
Purpose: organize leads, contacts, customer interactions, opportunities, pipeline stages, tasks, follow-ups, and reporting.
The supplied product description includes lead/contact management, deal tracking, calendars, dashboards, email integration, communication workflows, and configurable automation. Specific modules and integrations depend on the actual plan and implementation.
Best suited to scattered customer records, Excel-based sales tracking, missed follow-ups, unclear lead ownership, duplicate work, and poor pipeline visibility.
Example: a real-estate business organizes enquiries, assigns follow-up work, and tracks site-visit stages through configured workflows.
Simple explanation: “ZeaCRM helps your team manage leads, customer information, sales stages, and follow-ups in one place.”
Website, advertising, messaging, or third-party lead capture requires the corresponding integration; it is not automatically included.

## Zea Voice
Category: AI voice agents for customer conversations.
Purpose: incoming/outgoing calls, answers grounded in approved knowledge, lead qualification, connected appointment workflows, reminders, callbacks, and customer follow-up.
The current platform supports company-scoped contact history, individual call transcripts and summaries within a conversation thread, direction-specific prompts, and authorized follow-up scheduling. Availability depends on enabled features, agent settings, permissions, and deployment.
Company call capacity is shared across enabled calling flows. Excess outbound requests can wait in a queue; excess inbound callers can wait with hold music when enabled, subject to queue capacity and maximum wait. Do not promise unlimited capacity or a precise queue position without current data.
Best suited to missed calls, repetitive enquiries, high calling workload, slow follow-up, and appointment reminder tasks.
Example: a hospital agent answers approved administrative questions and checks/books appointment slots through connected tools. It does not provide medical diagnosis.
Simple explanation: “Zea Voice can handle customer conversations on incoming and outgoing calls and automate supported enquiry, appointment, and follow-up workflows.”
Scheduling requires an authorized saved task and an operating dispatch system. Customer request summaries alone do not trigger calls. Telephony, provider limits, credits, and configuration affect execution.
Appointment booking, WhatsApp delivery, CRM actions, and human transfer each require the corresponding configured integration. WhatsApp calling must not be advertised as currently available without verification. Languages and operating hours depend on configuration.

## Zea Play
Category: employee tasks, productivity, and gamified performance.
The supplied description includes task assignment/tracking, performance visibility, attendance, leave, communication, reports, dashboards, and gamification such as missions, points, levels, achievements, leaderboards, and rewards. Confirm which modules are available in the proposed plan.
Best suited to unclear task ownership, delayed work, weak progress visibility, and disconnected employee processes.
Example: a service company centralizes daily work assignments and gives managers visibility into pending and completed tasks.
Simple explanation: “Zea Play helps teams manage tasks and performance through an engaging employee experience.”

## Zea Brain
Category: company knowledge assistant.
Purpose: answer questions using accessible approved documents and connected knowledge sources, including SOPs, policies, manuals, product information, and onboarding material.
Best suited to scattered files, repeated internal questions, slow onboarding, and difficulty finding current information.
Example: staff ask questions about uploaded policies and receive answers grounded in accessible material, subject to permissions and retrieval quality.
Simple explanation: “Zea Brain helps your team find answers from approved company documents instead of searching files manually.”
Access to CRM, email, tasks, or external systems requires explicit connections and permissions. It does not automatically know every company record.

## Product matching and combinations
- Leads, customer records, and sales stages: ZeaCRM.
- Customer calling and connected appointment/follow-up workflows: Zea Voice.
- Employee tasks and performance: Zea Play.
- Answers from approved documents: Zea Brain.
Example combination: an enquiry enters ZeaCRM, Zea Voice makes an authorized call, a team task is created through an integration, and staff consult Zea Brain. Cross-product automation requires actual integration; no bundle of all four is mandatory.
