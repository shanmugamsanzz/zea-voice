# Zea AI Family — Product Knowledge Base

## Purpose

This document contains stable product knowledge only. Commercial values, plans, discounts, taxes, and setup charges are intentionally excluded; they belong exclusively in the Live Data pricing sheet.

## Company and product ecosystem

Zea AI Family is an AI business ecosystem from URL Factory Private Limited. It brings together four core solutions for sales, customer communication, employee productivity, and company knowledge:

- ZeaCRM
- Zea Voice
- Zea Play
- Zea Brain

The suite can be discussed as one connected ecosystem when a caller's need spans more than one area. Do not imply that every product or integration is included in every deployment.

## ZeaCRM

ZeaCRM is an AI CRM and business-automation platform for managing the customer and sales journey. It helps businesses centralize leads and customer records, manage sales pipelines and opportunities, organize quotations and invoices, automate follow-ups and business processes, track revenue, and monitor team performance.

It is relevant when a business has missed leads, inconsistent follow-ups, customer information in spreadsheets or separate tools, unclear pipeline visibility, or difficulty assigning and monitoring sales work.

Use simple positioning: ZeaCRM helps manage the journey from first enquiry through conversion and repeat business.

### ZeaCRM use cases

- Lead and contact management
- Lead assignment
- Sales-pipeline and deal tracking
- Follow-up management and follow-up automation
- Sales and customer-related task management
- Appointment management and demo booking
- Email and customer-communication tracking
- Quotation follow-up and payment reminders
- SLA tracking and escalation
- Customer activity and sales history
- Sales reporting, team-performance visibility, and workflow automation
- Customer retention, renewals, upselling, and cross-selling

## Zea Voice

Zea Voice is a 24×7 AI voice-agent solution for inbound and outbound customer conversations. It can answer calls, make calls, qualify leads, book or confirm appointments, handle enquiries, send reminders, automate repetitive calls, and connect conversation information with CRM where the approved project scope supports it.

It is relevant when a business misses calls, receives calls after hours, needs appointment or follow-up calls handled consistently, or wants to reduce repetitive calling work.

Use simple positioning: Zea Voice acts like an AI employee that can handle customer calls around the clock.

### Zea Voice use cases

- Inbound and outbound customer calls
- Lead qualification and lead follow-up
- Appointment booking, confirmation, and reminders
- Payment, renewal, and document reminders
- Configured customer enquiries and feedback collection
- Re-engagement and follow-up campaigns
- Human call transfer when required
- Industry-specific enquiry flows such as healthcare, real estate, automotive, education, and service businesses

## Zea Play

Zea Play is a gamified task and performance-management solution. It supports task assignment and tracking, accountability, productivity visibility, achievements, leaderboards, rewards, and manager oversight.

It is relevant when managers repeatedly chase task completion, daily work lacks visibility, team motivation is low, or performance tracking is manual.

Use simple positioning: Zea Play turns everyday work into missions so teams can be more engaged and accountable.

## Zea Brain

Zea Brain is a company-wide AI knowledge assistant. It makes company knowledge, documents, SOPs, policies, processes, and internal information easier to search and use through AI.

It is relevant when information is scattered, employees repeatedly ask the same internal questions, SOPs are difficult to find, or teams need a searchable knowledge layer across company information and approved business systems.

Use simple positioning: Zea Brain is like an AI assistant for a company's own knowledge.

### Zea Brain use cases

- Internal questions and company-policy questions
- SOP, process, and work-instruction guidance
- Finding and explaining company documents and terminology
- Product and department information
- Next-task guidance and employee training
- Connected live business information where available
- CRM, sales, lead, task, performance, growth, and management intelligence where the approved connection is available
- Knowledge transfer across teams

| Caller problem | Relevant solution |
| --- | --- |

## Conversation discovery guidance

Use a short, natural discovery path before recommending a Zea product:

1. Understand the caller's business type.
2. Understand their current business problem.
3. Understand how they currently handle that problem.
4. Match the stated problem to the relevant Zea product or products.

Do not turn this into a long qualification questionnaire. Do not ask for team size, user count, lead volume, call volume, budget, role, or implementation urgency unless it is strictly required for a configured action or the caller brings it up.

For a demo request, collect name and phone number first, then preferred date and time. Check the requested time through the configured **Appoinment Slot Checking** tool before offering or confirming a booking. Use the configured **Appoinment Booking** tool only after the caller selects an available slot and clearly confirms the booking.

When a caller asks to receive a brochure, catalog, product details, or company location on WhatsApp, use the relevant configured webhook/tool. Never state that information has been sent until the configured tool confirms success.

Do not assume an integration, customization, replacement of existing software, or technical scope before it has been reviewed. Solutions can be configured around a business process, but custom development, enhancements, and integrations depend on the approved scope.

## Scope boundary for operational requirements

If a caller describes an operational need that is not explicitly listed above, do not present an unverified capability as a standard product feature. First understand the workflow. Then explain the applicable approved product capability, if any, and state that any remaining requirement needs a business and technical scope review.
