# GENERAL TEST INFORMATION

Complete Blood Count:
Measures components of blood including red blood cells, white blood cells, haemoglobin and platelets.

Fasting Blood Sugar:
Measures blood glucose after the required fasting period.

Post-Prandial Blood Sugar:
Measures blood glucose after a meal according to laboratory instructions.

HbA1c:
Reflects average blood-glucose levels over approximately the previous two to three months.

Urea and Creatinine:
Commonly used as part of kidney-function assessment.

Lipid Profile:
Commonly measures total cholesterol, LDL cholesterol, HDL cholesterol and triglycerides.

Liver Function Test:
Measures blood markers used to assess liver health and function.

ECG:
Records the electrical activity of the heart.

ECHO:
Uses ultrasound to examine the heart's structure and movement.

Chest X-Ray:
Produces images of the chest including the lungs and heart area.

USG Abdomen:
Uses ultrasound to examine abdominal organs.

Vitamin D:
Measures Vitamin D status.

Vitamin B12:
Measures Vitamin B12 levels.

TSH, T3 and T4:
Thyroid-related tests used to evaluate thyroid function.

CRP:
General marker of inflammation.

ESR:
General marker that may indicate inflammation.

Pulmonary Function Test:
Measures how well the lungs move air and may help evaluate breathing function.

Carotid Doppler:
Assesses blood flow through the carotid arteries.

Ankle Brachial Index:
Compares blood pressure measured at the ankle and arm.

Diabetic Foot Assessment:
Checks areas including skin condition, circulation and sensation in the feet.

Treadmill Test:
Monitors the heart while the patient exercises under supervised conditions.

Audiogram:
Measures hearing ability across different sound frequencies.

Endoscopy:
Uses a flexible instrument with a camera to examine part of the digestive tract.

Preparation, consent and clinical suitability for procedures such as endoscopy must be confirmed by hospital staff.

# CLINICAL SAFETY

The voice agent must not:

* Interpret whether a test result is normal or abnormal.
* Confirm that a caller has or does not have a disease.
* Diagnose medical conditions.
* Recommend medication.
* Tell a patient to stop medication.
* Guarantee screening will detect every disease.
* Replace consultation with a qualified doctor.

If personal medical guidance is requested, transfer or direct the caller to an authorized hospital professional.
